# Subhakamana Store Management System Blueprint

**System Name:** Subhakamana Store Management System  
**Short Name:** SSMS  
**Store:** Subhakamana Store  
**Prepared Date:** July 3, 2026  
**Purpose:** Simple store system for daily use  

---

## 1. Simple Summary

Subhakamana Store Management System is a single system for running Subhakamana Store.

It helps the store manage:

- Billing
- Stock
- Products
- Customers
- Online orders
- Delivery
- Payments
- Purchases
- Staff access
- Reports

The goal is simple: keep every sale, product, customer, and payment in one place.

---

## 2. Who Will Use It

| User | What They Do |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Checks sales, profit, stock, payments, reports, and store performance. |
| Admin | Manages products, orders, customers, purchases, delivery, and reports. |
| Inventory Staff | Adds products, receives stock, updates quantities, and prints labels. |
| Sales Staff | Uses POS, creates bills, takes payments, and handles customers. |
| Delivery Staff | Packs orders, creates delivery records, and updates tracking status. |

---

## 3. Main Store Work

### Billing / POS

Staff can scan or search products, add them to cart, choose payment method, create bill, and reduce stock automatically.

### Stock

Staff can see which products are available, low in stock, or sold out.

### Products

The store can save product name, category, size, color, price, SKU, barcode, and photos.

### Customers

The store can save customer name, phone, address, purchase history, wishlist, and loyalty points.

### Online Store

Customers can view products online, add to cart, checkout, pay, and track orders.

### Purchases

Staff can record supplier bills and new stock arrivals.

### Delivery

Staff can prepare orders, assign courier, save tracking number, and update delivery status.

### Payments

The system can track cash, QR, bank transfer, eSewa, Khalti, Fonepay, IME Pay, card, wallet, and cash on delivery.

### Reports

The owner can check sales, stock value, profit, payment status, top products, slow products, and delivery status.

---

## 4. Store Helper

The store helper should answer simple questions using store data.

Examples:

- Do we have this in Medium?
- What colors are available?
- What arrived today?
- Which products are low in stock?
- What should we reorder?
- Which products are not selling?
- How much did we sell today?

Important rule: if the system does not know the answer, it should say staff must confirm it. It should not guess.

---

## 5. Simple Safety Rules

- Owner has full access.
- Admin has access to daily business work.
- Inventory staff can manage stock and products.
- Sales staff can bill customers and handle orders.
- Delivery staff can manage delivery updates.
- Important changes should be recorded.
- Store data should be backed up.
