Daily Staff Manual

  1. Start from Dashboard: choose the page for staff work or open Website Preview for customer-side review.
  2. Check low stock: open Dashboard or Inventory and review items marked Low Stock or Out of Stock.
  3. Add a product: open Products, enter category, size, color, prices, stock, take a phone photo or choose product images, then save the product variant.
  4. Receive stock: open Purchases to record supplier bills, then update Inventory when goods are received.
  5. Sell in store: open Billing Counter, scan or search SKU/barcode, choose customer and payment method, then complete sale.
  6. Process online order: open Orders, confirm payment and order status, then create delivery from the Delivery page.
  7. Track delivery: use Delivery to create local shipment records and monitor Pickup Pending, In Transit, Delivered, or Returned status.
  8. Review performance: open Reports for sales, payments, inventory value, COD, and export-ready business summaries.

Product Stock Workflow

  1. Supplier: choose the supplier that provides the item.
  2. Purchase Order: create a purchase order for the expected stock.
  3. Receive Stock: confirm actual received quantity.
  4. Create or Find Product: create a new product or open an existing product record.
  5. Create Variants: add size, color, variant SKU, and variant stock.
  6. Generate SKU + Barcode: create scannable product codes for stock and billing.
  7. Print Labels: print and attach barcode labels to products.
  8. Update Inventory: confirm stock appears correctly in inventory.
  9. Take Photos: use Take Photo With Phone on mobile, or take clear product photos separately.
  10. Upload Product Images: attach camera photos or saved images in Product Entry.
  11. Website Preview: check the product page before publishing.
  12. Online Ready: confirm price, stock, images, and description.
  13. Publish: make the item visible for selling.
  14. Counter / Online Sale: sell through barcode billing or online checkout.
  15. Stock Reduction: reduce stock after each sale.
  16. Reports / Returns / Audit: review sales, returns, and stock accuracy.

Important Rules

Inventory

Every sale, purchase, return, damaged item, or manual correction should create a stock movement record.

Payments

Always select the correct payment method: Cash, eSewa, Khalti, Bank Transfer, or Cash on Delivery.

Delivery

Confirm phone, city, address, COD amount, courier, and tracking number before dispatch.

Numerical Analysis Manual

  1. Inventory Value: Cost Price x Current Stock. This shows how much money is currently tied up in stock.
  2. Gross Profit: Sales Revenue - Cost of Goods Sold. This shows approximate profit before rent, salary, delivery, and other expenses.
  3. Gross Margin: Gross Profit / Sales Revenue x 100. This shows how much of each sale remains after product cost.
  4. Average Order Value: Sales Revenue / Number of Orders. This helps understand how much customers spend per order.
  5. Sell-Through Rate: Units Sold / (Units Sold + Current Stock) x 100. This helps show how quickly stock is moving.
  6. Reorder Need: Products where Current Stock is less than or equal to Reorder Level. This helps staff know what to purchase again.
