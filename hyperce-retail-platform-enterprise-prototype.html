What The System Connects

AreaSimple Meaning
ProductsAll sari, kurtha, blouse, dupatta, and other item details stay in one catalog.
StockWhen an item is sold or received, the quantity updates in the system.

ReportsSales, stock, payments, and delivery records become simple summaries for owners.

Important Store Rules

One Stock Count

Shop sales and online orders should use the same stock number so items are not oversold.

Every Change Recorded

Sales, returns, new stock, damaged goods, and corrections should be saved with a reason.

Right Person, Right Access

Owners, admins, inventory staff, and sales staff should only see the work they need.

Customer Help

Answer product questions

The helper can answer simple questions about size, color, price, matching items, new arrivals, and stock availability using store data.

Select a sample question to see how the helper should answer.

Owner Help

Answer business questions

The owner can ask what to reorder, what is not selling, which items made profit, and how the store is performing.

Select a sample insight to preview business intelligence output.

User Access

Owners, admins, inventory staff, and sales staff should have separate access levels.

Activity History

Important actions like stock edits, refunds, discounts, and delivery changes should be recorded.

Backup

Store data should be backed up so product, sales, and customer records are not lost.

Supported Payment Methods

Payment Checking

Manual

Staff can check bank transfer or QR payment proof and mark the order as paid.

Automatic

In the future, eSewa, Khalti, Fonepay, and other services can confirm payment automatically.

Report

Paid, pending, refunded, and cash-on-delivery amounts should appear in reports.
